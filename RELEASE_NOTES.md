## Scrolling up follows your hand again

### Fixed in 0.6.4

- Repeated wheel, trackpad and touch scrolling is no longer undone by transcript layout updates. The conversation moves on every gesture instead of sticking, fighting the input, or jumping back down when you stop.
- Earlier pages still arrive above the section you are reading without moving that section on screen.

This is a focused hotfix for the scroll regression in 0.6.3.
